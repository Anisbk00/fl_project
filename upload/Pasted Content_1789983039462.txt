NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

1. Instrumentation must never become a data leak.

Logs, metrics, traces, analytics, error events, alerts, CSP reports, screenshots, test artifacts, source maps, and reports must exclude:

- secrets;
- buyer emails;
- unnecessary raw IP addresses;
- access, session, consent, and unsubscribe tokens;
- URL fragments;
- signed URLs;
- private object paths;
- promotion codes;
- raw webhook bodies and signatures;
- encryption material;
- payment or provider identifiers linked to buyers;
- legal, privacy, or contact-form contents.

2. Use allowlisted structured telemetry fields with centralized redaction and automated leakage tests.

3. Observability must remain provider-neutral at the application boundary. Use OpenTelemetry where supported, a typed structured logger, and a replaceable error-monitoring adapter.

4. Monitoring must never authorize payments, fulfillment, downloads, admin access, policy publication, or promotion redemption. Durable Supabase state remains the source of truth.

5. Security controls must be layered. Vercel Firewall/WAF and bot controls may reduce edge traffic, but database constraints, RLS, authorization, validation, signature verification, quotas, idempotency, and durable rate limits remain authoritative.

6. Do not solve abuse by blocking accessibility tools, shared networks, privacy tools, legitimate webhook retries, or users unable to complete visual puzzles.

7. CAPTCHA or third-party challenges may be used only as progressive escalation after privacy and accessibility review.

8. Treat Content Security Policy as a tested security control. Do not paste a generic policy or make cacheable public pages dynamic unnecessarily.

9. Performance testing must use synthetic data and approved non-production infrastructure. Never load-test live Stripe, Resend, buyer inboxes, or an unapproved production Supabase project.

10. Target WCAG 2.2 AA using automated and manual testing. Automated tools alone do not prove accessibility.

11. A backup is not a recovery test. Perform an isolated restore drill covering database state, Storage objects, external-provider reconciliation, idempotency, and measured RPO/RTO.

12. Keep production release and live Stripe charging fail-closed.
