CURRENT ARCHITECTURE

The application uses:

- Next.js App Router with strict TypeScript;
- Supabase Postgres, Auth, and Storage;
- Stripe-hosted one-time guest Checkout;
- Resend behind a provider-neutral transactional-email interface;

- private deliverables separated from public audio previews;

- immutable purchased-product and deliverable snapshots;
- secure fragment-based package-access links;
- private Supabase Storage with short-lived signed URLs.

Step 6 already grants immutable entitlements and queues a secure package-access email automatically after webhook-confirmed payment, normally reaching the email provider in under 60 seconds. Preserve and regression-test that entire flow.

This step adds trustworthy public information and carefully bounded growth features. It does not authorize live charging, production deployment, behavioral advertising, bulk marketing campaigns, buyer accounts, loyalty programs, subscriptions, marketplace sellers, or AI-generated reviews.

Steps 8 and 9 must remain pending.

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

1. Treat legal text as jurisdiction-dependent content requiring qualified human review. Build the versioning, publishing, evidence, and release-gate system, but never invent final legal wording or claim legal compliance.

2. Preserve immutable order, consent, price, license, bundle, promotion, deliverable, and policy evidence. New policy revisions must never rewrite evidence attached to previous checkouts or orders.

3. Never create fake reviews, ratings, testimonials, customer counters, urgency, scarcity, countdowns, “recent purchase” popups, crossed-out prices, savings claims, bestseller claims, security badges, or compatibility claims.

4. Marketing consent must be separate, optional, specific, unchecked, and never required to purchase or receive a free product.

5. Transactional fulfillment email must never depend on marketing consent.

6. Do not introduce non-essential cookies or trackers in this step. Do not show a fake cookie banner when the site only uses strictly necessary storage.

7. All prices and discounts use integer minor units and explicit supported currencies. The browser must never control prices, discounts, eligibility, bundle contents, consent versions, review verification, or recommendation results.

8. Every public mutation must be schema validated, size bounded, abuse controlled, durably rate limited, non-enumerating, and protected against CSRF or cross-origin misuse where applicable.

9. Public catalog caching and performance must remain intact. Personalized or capability-bound information must never enter shared caches.

10. Keep live Stripe charging and production launch fail-closed. Missing legal review, merchant details, market rules, promotion reconciliation, or security evidence remains a release blocker.
